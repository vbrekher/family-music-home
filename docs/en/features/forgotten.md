# Forgotten Albums

[English](../../en/features/forgotten.md) · [Русский](../../ru/features/forgotten.md)

Revisit albums already in your collection using local listening history and favorites, without an external recommendation service.

## Before you start

Sign in to your own instance. Music selections require an indexed library you are allowed to read. No music is downloaded or purchased by these family tools.

## Use it

1. Open Family Home → Customize Home.
2. Enable forgotten album suggestions and the Forgotten Albums widget. Choose days since last listen and a rediscovery mode.
3. Save Home. Open an album or choose Not interested to dismiss that suggestion for your account.

## Check the result and change it

Reopen the page and confirm the saved state. For editable records, open your own record to edit it; use archive controls when available. If access changes, unavailable music is redacted rather than granting new access. If saving fails, keep your draft, reload the latest revision and retry. Empty libraries are valid: add or scan your music first.

## Permissions and privacy

Suggestions and dismissals are personal and filtered by current library permissions.

## Limits and troubleshooting

A new or empty collection may produce no suggestions. The weekly surprise mode is deterministic; it is not an AI judgment about musical taste.
