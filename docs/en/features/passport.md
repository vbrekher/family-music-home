# Music Quality Passport

[English](../../en/features/passport.md) · [Русский](../../ru/features/passport.md)

Inspect known technical metadata and diagnostic evidence for one recording.

## Before you start

Sign in to your own instance. Music selections require an indexed library you are allowed to read. No music is downloaded or purchased by these family tools.

## Use it

1. Open Quality passport.
2. Search your library and select a recording.
3. Inspect codec, bitrate, sample rate, bit depth, duration, source type and diagnostic state. Select another recording to compare its facts.

## Check the result and change it

Reopen the page and confirm the saved state. For editable records, open your own record to edit it; use archive controls when available. If access changes, unavailable music is redacted rather than granting new access. If saving fails, keep your draft, reload the latest revision and retry. Empty libraries are valid: add or scan your music first.

## Permissions and privacy

Only currently authorized catalog references resolve. Secret source paths, credentials and remote object URLs are not part of the passport.

## Limits and troubleshooting

Unknown means the information is unavailable. Hi-Res labeling does not establish audible superiority or original recording provenance. Reading a passport does not start a full audio analysis.
