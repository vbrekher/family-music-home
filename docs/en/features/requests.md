# Family Music Requests

[English](../../en/features/requests.md) · [Русский](../../ru/features/requests.md)

Keep a private wishlist, then send a request to the server administrators. Track progress for individual songs, albums or artists.

## Before you start

Sign in to your own instance. Music selections require an indexed library you are allowed to read. No music is downloaded or purchased by these family tools.

## Use it

1. Open Music requests and choose Create music request.
2. Enter Request title and Details and notes. Choose a request type; for a list enter one title or artist per line, up to 100.
3. Save draft, check the selected items and submit. Administrators review item statuses; the requester can inspect status history and cancel unneeded items.

## Check the result and change it

Reopen the page and confirm the saved state. For editable records, open your own record to edit it; use archive controls when available. If access changes, unavailable music is redacted rather than granting new access. If saving fails, keep your draft, reload the latest revision and retry. Empty libraries are valid: add or scan your music first.

## Permissions and privacy

Drafts remain private. Submitted requests go to server-derived administrator recipients; regular members cannot perform administrator status transitions.

## Limits and troubleshooting

No automatic purchases or downloads occur. Optional HTTPS listing URLs are validated and stored but never fetched by the request feature.
