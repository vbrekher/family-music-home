# Version Collector

[English](../../en/features/versions.md) · [Русский](../../ru/features/versions.md)

Keep related editions together while preserving separate catalog albums and files; choose your own preferred edition.

## Before you start

Sign in to your own instance. Music selections require an indexed library you are allowed to read. No music is downloaded or purchased by these family tools.

## Use it

1. Open Editions. Review suggested MusicBrainz release-group matches or create a manual group.
2. Select the albums. For manual grouping, confirm that you checked they are editions of the same release group.
3. Save the group and choose your preferred version. Reopen it to verify the preference; edit or archive only your own group.

## Check the result and change it

Reopen the page and confirm the saved state. For editable records, open your own record to edit it; use archive controls when available. If access changes, unavailable music is redacted rather than granting new access. If saving fails, keep your draft, reload the latest revision and retry. Empty libraries are valid: add or scan your music first.

## Permissions and privacy

Catalog permissions are rechecked. Preferences are per user and cannot grant access to an otherwise private library.

## Limits and troubleshooting

Identical album titles alone do not prove edition identity. The tool never merges originals, changes tags or removes another version.
