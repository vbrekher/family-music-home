# Library Doctor

[English](../../en/features/doctor.md) · [Русский](../../ru/features/doctor.md)

Audit catalog metadata first, then explicitly investigate a bounded selection for exact duplicates, acoustic similarity and decode problems.

## Before you start

Sign in to your own instance. Music selections require an indexed library you are allowed to read. No music is downloaded or purchased by these family tools.

## Use it

1. As an administrator, open Library Doctor and start a metadata window with explicit record and library limits.
2. Review findings. Before deep analysis, configure the local Doctor companion, source budgets and selected track IDs.
3. Start the selected diagnostic job. Pause, resume or cancel as needed; export diagnostic JSON to keep the evidence.

## Check the result and change it

Reopen the page and confirm the saved state. For editable records, open your own record to edit it; use archive controls when available. If access changes, unavailable music is redacted rather than granting new access. If saving fails, keep your draft, reload the latest revision and retry. Empty libraries are valid: add or scan your music first.

## Permissions and privacy

Administrator only. Protected child accounts cannot access diagnostic jobs or diagnostic audio leases.

## Limits and troubleshooting

Original audio is never rewritten or deleted. SHA-256 equality is exact byte evidence; Chromaprint similarity is qualified evidence, not proof of the same master. Spectral flags are not proof of lossy provenance. Source/offline quotas and job bounds apply.
