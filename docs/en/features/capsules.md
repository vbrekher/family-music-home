# Family Music Time Capsule

[English](../../en/features/capsules.md) · [Русский](../../ru/features/capsules.md)

Keep a dated memory with selected music, notes, private photos and short voice recordings.

## Before you start

Sign in to your own instance. Music selections require an indexed library you are allowed to read. No music is downloaded or purchased by these family tools.

## Use it

1. Open Memories and choose Create capsule.
2. Enter Title, Notes, Memory date and an IANA timezone, for example Europe/Paris. Select music and add permitted photo or voice files.
3. Choose a visibility stage and optional sharing recipients. Save, then open the memory to verify its text, music and attachments.

## Check the result and change it

Reopen the page and confirm the saved state. For editable records, open your own record to edit it; use archive controls when available. If access changes, unavailable music is redacted rather than granting new access. If saving fails, keep your draft, reload the latest revision and retry. Empty libraries are valid: add or scan your music first.

## Permissions and privacy

Private by default; explicit recipients and current library access are checked again when reading attachments.

## Limits and troubleshooting

Photos are normalized and size-limited; voice is limited to 120 seconds. There are per-entry, per-user and total storage limits. Originals in the music library are untouched.
