# Family Shelves

[English](../../en/features/shelves.md) · [Русский](../../ru/features/shelves.md)

Arrange music into personal collections, or explicitly share a shelf with selected people. A shelf contains references, not extra audio files.

## Before you start

Sign in to your own instance. Music selections require an indexed library you are allowed to read. No music is downloaded or purchased by these family tools.

## Use it

1. Open Shelves and choose Create shelf.
2. Enter Shelf title and Description. Search your library and select tracks, albums or playlists; use the move controls to order them.
3. Leave Share with empty for a private shelf, or choose recipients. Select a cover from your choices and choose Save shelf.

## Check the result and change it

Reopen the page and confirm the saved state. For editable records, open your own record to edit it; use archive controls when available. If access changes, unavailable music is redacted rather than granting new access. If saving fails, keep your draft, reload the latest revision and retry. Empty libraries are valid: add or scan your music first.

## Permissions and privacy

Only the owner edits the shelf. A shared recipient can read allowed content; an administrator does not gain access to another adult’s private shelf.

## Limits and troubleshooting

Editing with an old revision is rejected rather than overwriting newer work. Archive/unarchive changes the collection record and never deletes the original music.
