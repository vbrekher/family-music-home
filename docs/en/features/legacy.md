# Family Legacy Export

[English](../../en/features/legacy.md) · [Русский](../../ru/features/legacy.md)

Download a portable copy of your own music history and permitted shared records, then verify its inventory and SHA-256 checksums.

## Before you start

Sign in to your own instance. Music selections require an indexed library you are allowed to read. No music is downloaded or purchased by these family tools.

## Use it

1. Open Legacy archive. Choose Archive scope, optional UTC creation dates and whether to include private attachments, catalog summaries and playlists.
2. Choose Prepare private archive, then download before the short-lived link expires. Store the ZIP privately.
3. Use Verify a saved archive to select the downloaded ZIP. Read the integrity result before relying on the archive.

## Check the result and change it

Reopen the page and confirm the saved state. For editable records, open your own record to edit it; use archive controls when available. If access changes, unavailable music is redacted rather than granting new access. If saving fails, keep your draft, reload the latest revision and retry. Empty libraries are valid: add or scan your music first.

## Permissions and privacy

An administrator cannot export another adult’s private records. Permissions are checked again at download and attachment reads. The link belongs to the preparing account.

## Limits and troubleshooting

The ZIP is NOT encrypted and contains no original music. It includes JSON/Markdown/M3U and checksums. Verification is read-only, with no in-place import. Full encrypted server recovery is a separate backup procedure. Large scopes must be narrowed to respect archive bounds.
