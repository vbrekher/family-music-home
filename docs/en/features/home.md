# Family Home Dashboard

[English](../../en/features/home.md) · [Русский](../../ru/features/home.md)

Your own starting point for music, shelves, unread letters and rediscovery. It uses existing catalog references rather than copying audio.

## Before you start

Sign in to your own instance. Music selections require an indexed library you are allowed to read. No music is downloaded or purchased by these family tools.

## Use it

1. Open Family Home from the main menu.
2. Choose Customize Home. Enable the widgets you need and use Up/Down to arrange them.
3. Choose Save Home. Open a shelf or album; use the ordinary player to listen.

## Check the result and change it

Reopen the page and confirm the saved state. For editable records, open your own record to edit it; use archive controls when available. If access changes, unavailable music is redacted rather than granting new access. If saving fails, keep your draft, reload the latest revision and retry. Empty libraries are valid: add or scan your music first.

## Permissions and privacy

Each user owns their Home preferences. Shared entries and music remain subject to current library permissions.

## Limits and troubleshooting

Rediscovery is opt-in. A player queue is not automatically started. An unavailable source can leave catalog entries visible but unplayable.
