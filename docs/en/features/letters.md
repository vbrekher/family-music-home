# Music Letters

[English](../../en/features/letters.md) · [Русский](../../ru/features/letters.md)

Send a private musical note now or at a scheduled time, with selected recordings and optional attachments.

## Before you start

Sign in to your own instance. Music selections require an indexed library you are allowed to read. No music is downloaded or purchased by these family tools.

## Use it

1. Open Music letters and choose Create letter.
2. Enter a title and message, select Recipients and music. For later delivery, set the delivery time and IANA timezone.
3. Save the draft, review it, then send. Use the inbox/sent views to check delivery and read state.

## Check the result and change it

Reopen the page and confirm the saved state. For editable records, open your own record to edit it; use archive controls when available. If access changes, unavailable music is redacted rather than granting new access. If saving fails, keep your draft, reload the latest revision and retry. Empty libraries are valid: add or scan your music first.

## Permissions and privacy

Drafts are private to their author. Scheduled letters are hidden from recipients until delivery. Recipient access and attachment authorization are enforced on the server.

## Limits and troubleshooting

The application inbox is the delivery channel; email or push delivery is not promised. The server catches up overdue delivery after restart. Archiving a letter does not delete music.
